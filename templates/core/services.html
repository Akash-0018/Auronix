Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and process

How long does it take to complete a website?

The timeline varies depending on project complexity. Basic websites typically take 2-4 weeks, while complex projects may require 5-8 weeks. During our initial consultation, we'll provide a detailed timeline based on your specific requirements.

Do you provide ongoing support after launch?

Yes, we offer various support packages. All our plans include initial support, and we provide ongoing maintenance services to keep your website secure, updated, and performing optimally.

Can you work with existing websites or designs?

Absolutely. We frequently work with existing websites to improve performance, update designs, add new features, or migrate to new platforms while preserving existing content and SEO value.

What technologies do you use?

We use modern technologies including HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (React, Vue.js), Python (Django), and various CMS platforms. We choose the right technology stack based on your project requirements.

Do you offer custom development beyond websites?

Yes, we specialize in custom web applications, APIs, mobile solutions, and complex integrations. Our team can develop tailored solutions to meet specific business needs and workflows.
